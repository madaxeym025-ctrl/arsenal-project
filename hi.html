Our Rich History

The Invincibles (2003-2004)

Arsenal Football Club made history by winning the Premier League title without losing a single match. Under Arsène Wenger, the team played 38 games, won 26, and drew 12.

The Stadium Move

In 2006, Arsenal moved from their historic home, Highbury, to the ultra-modern Emirates Stadium, opening a new chapter for the club's future.
